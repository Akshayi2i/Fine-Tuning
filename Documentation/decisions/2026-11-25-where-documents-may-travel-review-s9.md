# Where documents may travel (review S9)

- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Decided by:** the user, on the options written up for the review
- **Where it is written:** azure_service/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md

## The decision

Two clouds: documents on Azure, page images and PDFs to the model servers on RunPod, with the conditions the code cannot enforce on a checklist a named person signs before go-live.

## Options refused

Serving the model inside Azure, so no document leaves it (a new serving setup).

## What would reopen it

A client contract or legal review requiring documents to stay in Azure, or a checklist condition that cannot be met.
